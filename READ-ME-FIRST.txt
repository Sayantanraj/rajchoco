========================================
  FOR CHOCO 🍫 — how to make it yours
========================================

TO OPEN IT:  double-click  index.html

Everything you'd want to change is at the TOP of  script.js
in the block marked "1. CONTENT". Open it in Notepad and edit
the text between the quote marks. Nothing else needs touching.

WHAT'S IN THERE
---------------
PHOTOS    - the 16 photos + the handwritten caption under each one.
            Change any "cap:" text to a real memory from that photo.
            That's the single biggest upgrade you can make.

TIMELINE  - "The Story Of Us". Your real journey: the first Instagram
            message (18 Jan 2025), Saraswati Pujo in Serampore, the month
            apart, Durga Pujo, the Diwali storm, and getting the job.
            This is the part she'll re-read.

REASONS   - 6 flip cards (3 per row on desktop). Add your inside jokes here.

STATS     - the funny Choco Meter bars.

LETTER    - the typed-out love letter. Rewrite it in your own words if
            you want — keep the line breaks (blank line between paragraphs).

QUOTES    - the rotating quotes.

BIRTHDAY  - { month: 9, day: 16 } — the countdown auto-targets the next
            16 September, and on the day itself the whole page switches
            to "IT'S TODAY!!!" and throws confetti by itself.

WHAT SHE CAN DO ON THE PAGE
---------------------------
 - tap the gift box to open the site
 - tap any photo to blow it up (arrow keys work too)
 - flip all 6 reason cards
 - try to click "No" on "Do you love me?" (good luck, it runs away)
 - tap the cake to blow out the candles -> confetti + balloons
 - 🎵 button plays / pauses your song (17.mp3) — it also starts by itself
   when she opens the gift. To use a different song, replace 17.mp3 or change
   SONG_FILE in script.js
 - 🎉 button throws confetti whenever she wants

SENDING IT TO HER
-----------------
The folder must stay together — index.html, style.css, script.js and all
16 .jpeg files AND 17.mp3 in the SAME folder. Zip the whole folder and send it, or
ask me to publish it as a private link she can open on her phone.
